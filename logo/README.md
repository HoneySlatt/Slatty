# Slatty's emblem

`slatty.svg` is Slatty's emblem: a gothic S in a cracked ring, a small flame at its foot.

It is not under the code's licence, GPL-3.0-or-later: its author keeps its rights
([ADR-0035](../docs/adr/0035-slattys-emblem.md)). You may show it, unchanged, to refer to Slatty:
in an article, a review or a talk. You may ship it, unchanged, with Slatty when you package it. You
may not use it, or something close to it, as the emblem of another project, a fork of Slatty
included, nor so as to suggest that a project is Slatty or endorsed by it.

The older emblem in the program, `crates/gui/assets/logo.svg`, and the package's icons stay under
the GPL, as released; this one replaces them at Stage 5.
